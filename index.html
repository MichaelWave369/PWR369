Important distinction

The point field borrows visual vocabulary from the user's description of RGB “fuzzy dots,” nested rings and gears. The animation is not a recording of the heart, brain, electromagnetic fields or other realms.

Real heart, breathing and brain rhythms have measurable interactions. Their exact relationship to a person's inner visual imagery requires independent evidence.
